# Data

Data documentation and replication inputs will be added here in subsequent releases.

The study uses fossil-fuel carbon dioxide emissions and energy consumption from the U.S. Energy Information Administration's Monthly Energy Review, electric-plant fuel receipt costs from the same publication, and market returns and financial predictors from the Goyal-Welch data collection. Supplementary comparisons also use the Kenneth R. French Data Library and industrial production from the Federal Reserve / FRED.

Each release will document the retained source versions, original units, transformations, sample dates, and information timing. Data remain attributed to their original providers. Source links and access instructions will accompany materials that are obtained directly from those providers.
